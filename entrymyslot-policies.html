01 Privacy Policy

EntryMySlot respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information used through our website, mobile applications and booking services. This policy applies to the EntryMySlot platform and its movie, turf and event booking services.

By using EntryMySlot, you acknowledge that information may be collected and processed as described in this policy. We aim to collect and use information only for legitimate platform, booking, security, support and legal purposes.

02 Information We Collect

Account informationName, email address, mobile number and authentication/account details.
Booking informationMovie, turf and event bookings, ticket details, selected seats/resources, dates, times and booking status.
Device informationIP address, browser/device type, operating system, app version, access times and diagnostic information.
LocationApproximate or permission-based location when needed for location-aware features.
Support informationInformation you provide when contacting EntryMySlot support or reporting a problem.
Technical informationCookies, local storage, session information and similar technologies used to operate and secure the platform.

03 How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your EntryMySlot account.
  • Authenticate users and maintain secure sessions.
  • Process movie, turf and event bookings and issue tickets.
  • Send OTPs, booking confirmations, ticket information and security alerts.
  • Handle eligible organisation-side cancellations and refunds.
  • Provide customer support and resolve disputes or booking issues.
  • Improve platform reliability, performance and user experience.
  • Detect fraud, abuse, unauthorised activity and security threats.
  • Comply with applicable legal, regulatory and lawful requests.

04 Information Sharing

EntryMySlot does not sell personal information merely for the purpose of selling it. Information may be shared when reasonably necessary to provide the requested service.

  • Movie theatres: relevant booking information needed to fulfil a movie booking.
  • Turf organisations: relevant booking information needed to fulfil a turf booking.
  • Event organisations: relevant booking information needed to fulfil an event booking.
  • Payment providers: information necessary to process or reconcile payments.
  • Communication/OTP providers: information necessary to send authentication or service messages.
  • Hosting, analytics and technical providers: limited information needed to operate and improve the platform.
  • Authorities and professional advisers: where legally required or reasonably necessary for legal, security or compliance purposes.

05 Security

EntryMySlot uses reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information, including HTTPS, authentication controls, password hashing, access restrictions, session/token security, infrastructure controls, monitoring and logging.

Important: No internet-connected service can guarantee absolute security. Users should keep passwords, OTPs, tickets and account credentials confidential and report suspected unauthorised activity promptly.

06 Terms of Use

By accessing EntryMySlot, you agree to use the platform lawfully and in accordance with these policies. You must provide accurate information, protect your account credentials and not misuse the platform, tickets, payment systems, APIs or other users' information.

EntryMySlot may restrict, suspend or terminate access where there is suspected fraud, abuse, security risk, unlawful activity, repeated policy violations or other legitimate platform-security reasons.

07 Booking Policy

EntryMySlot provides a platform through which users can discover and book movies, turfs and events offered by participating organisations.

No cancellation after booking: Once a booking has been successfully completed, the user cannot cancel the booking.

Users should verify the selected movie show and seat, turf date/time/resource, or event date/ticket details before completing payment.

08 Cancellation & Refund Policy

User-initiated cancellation

There is no user-initiated cancellation after a booking has been successfully completed. Accordingly, users should carefully review booking details before payment.

Organisation-side cancellation or service issue

If a cinema, turf organisation or event organisation cancels a booking, or an organisation-side issue prevents the booked service from being provided, an eligible refund may be processed according to the applicable booking circumstances.

Refund calculation for all three categories
Refund = eligible booking / ticket amount excluding GST + platform fee

This rule applies to movies, turfs and events. GST and the EntryMySlot platform fee are not included in the refund. The time taken for the refunded amount to reach the original payment method may vary depending on the payment gateway, bank, card network, UPI provider or other financial institution.

Where an organisation-side cancellation or issue makes a refund applicable, EntryMySlot will initiate the eligible refund according to the applicable process. Specific booking terms displayed at the time of purchase may also apply.

After ticket issuance

Once payment has been completed and the ticket has been issued, the booking cannot be cancelled, refunded or exchanged by the user except where an eligible organisation-side cancellation or service issue applies.

09 Payments, GST & Platform Fee

Payments may be processed through third-party payment providers. EntryMySlot may receive payment status, transaction references and other information necessary to confirm and reconcile a booking.

Where applicable, GST and platform fees are displayed or incorporated into the payable amount according to the applicable transaction. For an eligible organisation-side refund, GST and platform fee are excluded from the refund.

Users should not share card PINs, UPI PINs, passwords or authentication secrets with EntryMySlot staff, organisations or any third party.

10 Tickets, QR Codes & Entry

After successful payment, EntryMySlot may issue a digital ticket, booking confirmation, QR code or other proof of booking. Users are responsible for presenting the required ticket or booking information at the venue.

Tickets should not be duplicated, altered, resold or used fraudulently. Entry requirements, venue rules and identity checks may be imposed by the relevant organisation.

11 Organisation / Partner Responsibility

Participating cinemas, turf operators and event organisations are responsible for the services, facilities, schedules, availability and venue-level operations they provide.

Where an organisation cancels or cannot provide a booked service, EntryMySlot may coordinate the applicable refund process under this policy. The refund calculation remains subject to the exclusion of GST and platform fee described above.

12 User Responsibility

  • Provide accurate account and booking information.
  • Review booking details before payment.
  • Keep login credentials, OTPs and tickets secure.
  • Follow venue, cinema, turf and event rules.
  • Do not misuse, manipulate or interfere with EntryMySlot or its booking systems.
  • Do not attempt fraudulent payment, duplicate-ticket use, unauthorised access or chargeback abuse.

13 Accounts & Age Requirement

EntryMySlot is intended for users aged 13 years and above. By creating or using an account, you represent that you meet the applicable minimum age requirement.

Users are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials and for activity performed through their account.

14 Account Deletion & Data Retention

Users may request deletion of their EntryMySlot account through the available account controls or by contacting privacy@entrymyslot.com. Where a deletion request is made, EntryMySlot will process the request subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, transaction-record and other legitimate retention requirements.

Certain records may need to be retained for legal compliance, accounting, dispute resolution, security, fraud prevention or transaction verification even after an account is deleted.

15 Cookies, Local Storage & Location

Cookies and local storage

EntryMySlot may use cookies, local storage, session technologies and similar mechanisms to maintain authentication, remember preferences, improve performance, secure the service and understand technical usage.

Location permission

EntryMySlot may request location permission for location-based features such as discovering relevant movies, turfs, events or services. Location permission can generally be controlled through the device or browser settings.

Where appropriate, access may be restricted and relevant information may be shared with payment providers, organisations, service providers or lawful authorities for investigation and protection of users and the platform.
